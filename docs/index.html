World Models for Physical AI: Interfaces, Experimental Evidence, and Downstream Utility

Learned world models predict how physical environments evolve, yet higher forecast fidelity does not automatically improve physical behavior. Forecasts enter Physical AI systems through distinct interfaces—training supervision, candidate-plan scoring, action-generation context, offline evaluation, or runtime correction—and each role requires its own experimental control.

01 · Core Findings & Analytical Framework

Prediction quality, decision utility, and execution reliability separate along specific links

Rather than grading architectures in the abstract, this survey audits what primary experiments actually vary and control across learning, planning, action generation, evaluation, and runtime intervention.

Finding 01 · Learning vs. Inference

Training-time future supervision aids policies even without deployment generation

Across objective ablations, removing future supervision hurts policy performance even when the deployed network reads only current observations. Deployment-time future reading, by contrast, has few isolated execution controls.

Fast-WAM Base: 91.8% vs. 83.8% (RoboTwin); DyPES-VLA & Cosmos Policy consistent
Finding 02 · Selection & Evaluation Reversals

Higher offline fit or evaluator correlation can reverse downstream

Test-time scoring gains depend first on mixed-outcome candidate headroom and second on closed-loop alignment. In evaluation, simulators ranking existing policies better can still train weaker new learners.

GeoBoN: 77.4% → 82.5% online reversal; DexTouch-WM: r=0.844 vs. lower X-VLA/π₀.₅
Finding 03 · Windows & Partial Progress

Runtime fixes and aggregate averages mask real-execution failures

For suffix-editable controllers, revisions must beat execution commitment without hurting net completion. Meanwhile, RoboTwin and RoboDojo averages conceal scene-randomization collapses and zero-completion progress credit.

RoboTwin: 32/33 mean drop; RoboDojo: 858/3,536 (24.3%) zero-SR partial credit

This Survey Physical AI (manipulation anchor; driving/nav. contrasts) 93 source-linked comparisons (75 studies) & frozen snapshots Links each predictive operation to execution endpoints, sensitivity bounds, and falsifiable tests
Test 01 · Planning (L5)

Physical same-state branching

Fixed controls
Restored scene, randomized branch order, candidate pool, continuation policy, search budget.
Varied intervention
Candidate scoring rule only.
Required outcomes
All-starts terminal completion difference (primary); mixed-outcome starts (diagnostic); K=1 budget reference; scoring latency.
Falsification criterion
Precise gain below prespecified threshold vs. equal-budget uniform selection argues against the scorer.
Test 02 · Contact Planning (L2, L4, L5)

Dynamics swap under frozen readout

Fixed controls
Sensors, frozen encoder, shared latent/physical state space, goal cost, search budget, replanning rule.
Varied intervention
Predictive dynamics model only.
Required outcomes
Rollout error, observed object response, closed-loop task completion, planning latency.
Falsification criterion
Lower rollout error without higher closed-loop completion isolates only a fidelity gain, not planning utility.
Test 03 · Runtime Control (L5, L6)

Runtime access to predicted contact

Fixed controls
Observations, base chunk & action index, compute budget, editable window, and decision deadline.
Varied intervention
Access to cached predictive reference.
Required outcomes
Net completion across all attempts, latency distribution, and missed-deadline rate.
Falsification criterion
Net gain below prespecified threshold—from weak revisions or late updates—counts against runtime predictive access.
